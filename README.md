# Undergrad_Projects

Policy Proposal Based on Risk Map Derivation to Eliminate Blind Spots of Elderly Welfare in Cheonan City
Project for the 2025 Cheonan City Data Analysis Idea Contest
Best Project, 2025 Summer Conference, Ewha Statistics Analysis Association
•	Collected demographic, economic, facility, and transportation data and processed them to construct 14 detailed indicators for each of the 31 administrative districts
•	Conducted factor analysis to derive 5 key factors, performed spatial autocorrelation analysis to identify Hot Spots, and applied K-Means clustering to classify districts into high-, medium-, and low-risk groups
•	Developed risk-based policy recommendations to reduce elderly welfare blind spots, highlighting areas for targeted interventions

Growth Strategy for Less-Active, High-Satisfaction Regions: Focused on Proposing Customized Wellness Tourism Policy Ideas
Project for the 3rd Big Data AI Society Alliance Data Analysis Contest (Awarded 2nd Place)
•	Segmented domestic tourists into seven clusters using PCA and K-Means based on 2024 National Travel Survey data.
•	Identified low-activity, high-satisfaction regions in Jeollanam-do using Tourism Activity Index, and developed a Wellness Tourism Index via Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA) across 22 municipalities to quantify regional wellness resources and tourism spending
•	Proposed tailored tourism policies and programs for top target regions, aligning local resources with tourist preferences to promote sustainable wellness tourism.
